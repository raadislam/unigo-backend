## Discovery and identity decision

Abkhazia is a de facto administered region with its own local consular and border-permit system. Georgia's official legislation treats the territory as the Autonomous Republic of Abkhazia within Georgia and regulates foreign entry into it. This record uses Georgia (GE) as the parent for UniGo's internationally recognised country-master structure; it does not make a claim that Abkhazia is a separately recognised sovereign country.

The local administration's Ministry of Foreign Affairs publishes its own visa-free list, entry-permit application process, consular fee schedule and local visa issuance procedure. These rules are materially different from the parent Georgia visa. A separate child-destination record could therefore be useful, but only if UniGo can represent the parent relationship, the legal route restrictions and the separate local permit without replacing or contradicting Georgia's country-level record.

## Bangladesh ordinary-passport assessment

### Local visa / entry permit

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Abkhazia's current entry-permit page states that a visa regime applies to citizens of foreign countries without a mutual visa-free agreement. Its published visa-free list names Russia, Nicaragua, Tuvalu, the Pridnestrovian Moldavian Republic and South Ossetia; Bangladesh is not listed. The page also describes a separate conditional exemption for foreign sightseers (except Georgian citizens) travelling in an organised excursion group for no more than 24 hours. That group exception is not a confirmed general tourism route for a Bangladesh ordinary-passport holder.

For applicants using the standard entry-permit process, the Ministry page requires:

- a copy of the applicant's passport, sent by email or fax;
- passport validity of at least six months before the planned entry;
- a completed electronic application form, with every field completed;
- submission to the Ministry's consular email address;
- an entry-permit decision sent to the application email address or fax number;
- a stated processing period of seven working days for the request to the Consular Department; this is an estimate, not a guarantee;
- appearance at the Consular Department within three working days after arrival to receive the local visa.

The Ministry says it issues single-entry and multiple-entry visas. The entry permit is therefore a prior approval step, not itself a complete visa or a guarantee of admission.

The official online form asks for citizenship, passport details, purpose and period of stay, transport and intended border-crossing location. The form labels a field “COPY OF DOCUMENTS IN JPG”; it does not establish whether this means a face photograph, a passport-document image, or both. Do not infer a photo specification from this field.

### Conditional organised-tour route

The same Ministry entry-permit page lists foreign sightseers, other than Georgian citizens, entering as part of an organised excursion group for a period not exceeding 24 hours as a category that may enter without obtaining a visa. It does not specify a Bangladesh-specific application procedure, the exact tour operator approval required, the lawful border route, or whether the exemption is available to every ordinary-passport nationality. Keep this as a conditional route requiring written confirmation, not as a general visa-free permission.

### Route and legal conflict

The Ministry's entry-permit page says the Abkhaz-Georgian border checkpoints operate from 08:00 to 19:00. Georgia's official Law on Occupied Territories states that foreign citizens may enter the Autonomous Republic of Abkhazia only from Zugdidi Municipality and that entry from any other direction is prohibited and punishable under Georgian criminal law.

A non-government tourism-information site, Discover Abkhazia, says Bangladesh is among the nationalities that need a double-entry or multiple-entry Russian visa when travelling through the Russian Federation. That statement is not an official Russian or Georgian source and must not be used as a confirmed Bangladesh route rule. It also does not resolve the conflict with Georgia's law.

**Do not advise a Bangladesh traveller to enter Abkhazia through Russia.** Before any route can be presented, obtain written confirmation from the Georgian authorities that the Zugdidi route is open to the relevant foreign visitor, and from the Abkhaz Consular Department that it will accept the same route and issue the required local permit. The parent Georgia visa requirement remains separate and must be satisfied before entering Georgia.

### Stay, validity, fees and processing

- **Local visa requirement:** Bangladesh is absent from the Ministry's published visa-free list. The standard local entry-permit/visa process appears to apply, but the Ministry must confirm the treatment of an ordinary Bangladesh passport and any 24-hour organised-tour exemption.
- **Passport validity:** at least six months before planned entry, according to the Ministry's entry-permit page.
- **Processing estimate:** seven working days for the entry-permit request. The page does not describe this as a guarantee. A local visa is to be collected within three working days after arrival.
- **Permitted stay / validity:** the entry-permit page requires the applicant to state the proposed stay dates and says the local visa may be single or multiple entry, but it does not establish a Bangladesh-specific maximum stay or issued visa validity.
- **Government fee:** the Ministry's consular-fee page is internally unclear for tourist visas. It lists RUB 350 for a tourist visa up to one month and RUB 700 up to six months, then lists RUB 2,100, RUB 4,200 and RUB 5,600 against additional duration rows without explaining the overlap. Do not publish a definitive fee until the Ministry confirms the applicable category and current amount in writing.
- **Payment:** the Ministry FAQ says visa payment is made at banks or by electronic card at POS terminals installed where the visa is issued. This is a local payment instruction, not a VAC fee.
- **Application channel:** email or fax for the passport copy and completed electronic form; the Ministry page names `visa@mfaapsny.org`. The permit response is returned by email or fax.
- **Transit:** no separate airside transit exemption was identified. Travel through Georgia or Russia is subject to each parent's immigration and legal rules; the local permit does not override them.
- **General tourism:** this is a legally contested, de facto administered region with a distinct local visa system and a route conflict. Do not describe the local permit as a substitute for Georgia or Russia entry permission.

2. Obtain written confirmation from the Georgian Ministry of Foreign Affairs / Border Police that the Zugdidi route is currently available to the relevant foreign visitor and confirm the required Georgian visa/entry documents for the proposed itinerary.
3. Obtain written confirmation from the Abkhaz Consular Department that it accepts entry via the Zugdidi route, and that the prior entry permit can be processed for a Bangladesh-resident applicant using that route.
4. Do not use the secondary Discover Abkhazia statement about a double-entry Russian visa as a production requirement until confirmed by an official Russian or Abkhaz authority. Do not advise entry through Russia because Georgia's official law prohibits foreign entry from directions other than Zugdidi.

6. Confirm whether “COPY OF DOCUMENTS IN JPG” requires a face photo in addition to the passport scan, and whether any printed photograph or biometric capture is required.
7. Approve a parent-child destination schema before creating a separate Abkhazia row. Keep the parent Georgia record unchanged and authoritative for Georgian immigration.
