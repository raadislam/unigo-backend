# Visa discovery batch 11 — expanded findings

Checked: 2026-11-14  
Passport: Bangladesh ordinary passport  
Purpose: short-stay tourism and destination access

This batch covers three destinations from the previously unstarted ledger. Findings are based on official government sources. A destination is not marked publishable until the country-specific application route and all required fields are confirmed.

| Destination | Confirmed rule and route | Fee / timing | Current decision and remaining work |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brazil | Bangladesh is marked VIVIS code 1 (visa required) in Brazil MFA's visit-visa table. The Brazilian Embassy in Dhaka accepts VIVIS tourism applications. It requires online application form, passport with at least six months validity, NID/birth certificate translated and notarized, photos, six-month bank statements and balance certificate, income-tax certificate/acknowledgement translated and notarized, recent utility bill, police clearance, accommodation, return ticket reservation, purpose/status letter and employment evidence. Original passport and supporting copies are submitted; appointment is required. | Embassy Dhaka lists a BDT 11,200 pay order for VIVIS; processing may take up to 20 working days. | Visa-required and local application route are confirmed. Before staging a full record, confirm whether the fee and checklist page is current and whether any current form/appointment requirements have changed. |
| Cyprus | Bangladesh ordinary passport is explicitly on Cyprus MFA's visa-required list. Bangladesh has no resident Cyprus diplomatic mission and is covered by the Cyprus High Commission in India. The High Commission's official page says VFS Global provides visa services for parallel-accreditation countries including Bangladesh; the listed VFS India portal is the application channel. A valid double/multiple-entry Schengen Category C visa may be accepted as equivalent for Cyprus short stays; qualifying EU/Schengen Category D visa or residence permit holders may also be exempt, subject to conditions. | Standard short-stay Category C fee is €90 (children 6–12: €45; under 6: free). Cyprus says applications should be lodged at least 15 days before travel and no earlier than six months before; normal decision time is 15 days and may extend to 45 days. VFS India service fee is listed separately at €2.60 excluding GST. | Visa-required and Bangladesh's VFS route are confirmed. Keep draft until the current Bangladesh-specific VFS checklist, fee collection currency and actual submission location/appointment process are confirmed. |
| Andorra | Andorra does not issue a visa and is not in Schengen; access is only through France or Spain. Andorra Government explicitly states that non-EU nationals who need a Schengen visa must hold a double-entry or multiple-entry Schengen visa, because entering Andorra means leaving Schengen and re-entering on return. | No Andorran visa fee. Schengen visa fee and processing depend on the French or Spanish mission with jurisdiction for the travel route. | Conditional-entry guidance only; not visa-free access for Bangladeshi ordinary-passport holders who need Schengen access. Do not create a standalone Andorran visa requirement. |
