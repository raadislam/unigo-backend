# Batch 47 — Isle of Man visitor entry for Bangladeshi ordinary passport holders

**Research date:** 15 November 2026  
**Destination:** Isle of Man (IM)  
**Nationality:** Bangladesh — ordinary passport  
**Purpose:** Tourism / visitor  
**Record operation:** Update the existing pending BGD tourism record; do not create a duplicate country or visa record.

## Decision

A Bangladeshi ordinary-passport visitor should obtain a valid UK Standard Visitor visa that covers the Isle of Man. The Isle of Man Government confirms that a valid visa issued by the UK Home Office/UKVI, Jersey or Guernsey permits a visit to the Isle of Man without a separate Isle of Man visa. Bangladesh appears on the current UK Home Office Appendix Visitor visa-national list. The Home Office's Bangladesh fee table explicitly names the Standard Visitor visa as covering the UK, Isle of Man, Jersey and Guernsey.

This is **not visa-free entry**, and there is no visa-on-arrival route in the researched visitor path. The record does not describe the Isle of Man as issuing its own eVisa: IOM-specific entry clearance is recorded digitally from 1 July 2026, but the Isle of Man Government expressly says that this digital status is not an eVisa. A UKVI-issued Standard Visitor visa is issued as a UK eVisa and is accepted by the Isle of Man as a qualifying visa.

## Entry route and conditions

- **Visa category:** UK Standard Visitor visa, valid for the Isle of Man.
- **Visa required:** Yes for a Bangladesh ordinary-passport visitor using the established UKVI route; a valid UKVI-issued visa allows travel to the Isle of Man without a further IOM visa.
- **Visa-free / visa on arrival:** No / No.
- **IOM eVisa:** No separate IOM eVisa. IOM-issued entry clearance status is digital but expressly not an eVisa.
- **Permitted stay:** Up to six months per visit under Standard Visitor permission, subject to the permission granted.
- **Validity / entries:** Standard Visitor options include 6 months, 2 years, 5 years and 10 years; each visit is capped at six months. The short-term fee schedule allows single or multiple entry; the actual grant controls.
- **Passport validity:** Valid for the whole intended stay. No additional six-month post-expiry rule is stated in the UKVI Standard Visitor application guidance.
- **Qualifying visa:** A valid visa issued by UK Home Office/UKVI, Jersey or Guernsey is accepted by the Isle of Man without a separate IOM visa. No broader third-country residence-permit exemption is asserted.
- **Visitor conditions:** Tourism and visiting friends/family are permitted; work and study are not permitted under ordinary visitor permission. The visitor must be able to support themselves and leave at the end of the visit.
- **Funds:** Sufficient accessible funds for the visit and return/onward journey; no fixed minimum balance published.
- **Accommodation:** Provide where the applicant will stay and the arrangements. A prepaid hotel booking is not a universal visa requirement.
- **Return travel:** Show intention and ability to leave and pay for return/onward travel; do not treat a purchased ticket as universally mandatory.
- **Insurance / health:** No general mandatory travel-insurance or health-certificate requirement was identified in the reviewed ordinary visitor guidance. Insurance is recommended; check itinerary-specific health advice.
- **Transit:** UK airport transit is governed by UK transit rules. A valid UKVI visitor visa also permits Isle of Man visits without a separate IOM visa. Irish-route exemptions are conditional and are not treated as a general Bangladesh exemption.

VFS optional services and any centre service charges are separate from the Home Office government fee.

The Isle of Man Immigration Service currently publishes an estimate of **up to 13 weeks** for visit applications made from outside the Isle of Man/UK. This is an estimate, not a guarantee. Do not replace it with the generic UKVI three-week Standard Visitor processing estimate because IOM-specific applications may be referred to the Isle of Man Immigration Service.

## Application channel in Bangladesh

1. Complete the Standard Visitor application online through GOV.UK/UKVI before travel.
2. Use the UKVI application route that covers the Isle of Man.
3. Book an appointment at a VFS UK visa application centre in Bangladesh. The VFS Bangladesh portal lists Dhaka and Sylhet centres; optional services are separately charged.
4. Attend the appointment with the passport and personalised checklist. UKVI states that fingerprints and a facial photograph are taken at the VAC, unless the applicant is exempt.
5. Supply the supporting documents generated by the application and relevant evidence of purpose, finances, accommodation and intention to leave.
6. If the applicant seeks Isle of Man-specific entry clearance rather than relying on an existing valid UKVI visa, confirm the current route with the Isle of Man Immigration Service before travel.

## Photo specification audit

**Status:** officially confirmed no fixed universal size in the reviewed Bangladesh biometric route; photograph captured at the VFS UK VAC.

GOV.UK states that the applicant's fingerprints and a photograph are taken at the visa application-centre appointment. The reviewed Bangladesh route does not publish a universal printed-photo size, digital pixel dimensions, file-size limit, file format, head-height measurement or number of printed copies for applicants who attend biometric enrolment. Do not invent these values.

A VAC photograph does not prove that a separate photo can never be required. Applicants exempt from biometric enrolment must follow the personalised UKVI/VFS instructions, which may request a recent photo. The public record therefore distinguishes captured_at_vac=true from no_fixed_size_published=true.

## Applicant checklist design

Four applicant profiles are supplied: employee, self-employed/business owner, student and retired. The common application steps are required; employment, business, study, pension and sponsor documents are conditional supporting evidence based on the applicant's circumstances. UKVI's supporting-document guide does not impose one universal pack or fixed bank balance. Certified translations are required for documents not in English or Welsh. The online form and personalised checklist remain controlling.
