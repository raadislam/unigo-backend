## Discovery and identity decision

Puntland is a self-governing federal member state/administration within Somalia. It is not a separate ISO 3166-1 country or territory. The Puntland Ministry of Interior, Federal Affairs and Democratization maintains an official government website and contact channel. Recent reporting attributes foreign entry-visa and residence-permit issuance to Puntland's Ministry of Security and reports a locally administered visa system called VISO, separate from the Federal Government of Somalia's eVisa/ETAS.

The existing UniGo country master represents Somalia as ISO `SO`. Keep that row unchanged. Track Puntland as a non-ISO child candidate because the reported local entry controls may materially differ from the federal Somalia route. Do not create a new ISO country row or merge an unconfirmed regional fee into the Somalia country-level visa requirement.

## Bangladesh ordinary-passport tourism assessment

### Entry route — unresolved conflict between federal and regional authorities

1. The Federal Government of Somalia Immigration and Citizenship Agency announced on **1 September 2025** that all foreign nationals travelling to Somalia must obtain entry authorisation online before travel through the federal eVisa/ETAS system. The official Federal Republic of Somalia Aeronautical Information Publication, effective **27 November 2025**, states that all visas are issued online and arriving passengers must obtain an Electronic Travel Authorization before travel. These are federal rules and do not prove that Puntland's regional authority accepts the federal authorisation at its own entry points.
2. Puntland's Ministry of Interior official website identifies the Ministry as the responsible regional government body and provides its official contact channel, but the official pages located during this research do not publish a current foreign-visitor visa circular, VISO application portal, nationality eligibility list, tourist checklist, fee schedule or stay conditions.
3. A **6 November 2025** report by Mustaqbal Media attributes to the Puntland Council of Ministers a decision assigning foreign entry permits and travel authorisations to Puntland's Ministry of Security, in direct opposition to the federal eVisa system. Caasimada Online reported the same Council decision and quoted the wording assigning visa and residence-permit services for foreign passports to Puntland's Ministry of Security. These are secondary reports of a government meeting, not the signed official Council decision.
4. Garowe Online reported on **5 November 2025** that Puntland had launched a local visa system called **VISO**, rejected the federal eVisa, and charged travellers **USD 60**. Other secondary reports described the same local fee and conflict. No official VISO portal, signed fee order, current fee schedule or current implementation notice was found. Do not treat USD 60 as a current official fee.
5. A 3 September 2026 first-person account published by Puntland Post describes a foreign-passport holder using a federal Somalia eVisa who was still asked to pay a local visa charge on arrival at Garowe. This is a single reported traveller experience, not a government rule or proof of the current policy for Bangladeshi tourists.

**Bangladesh-specific conclusion:** no primary official source reviewed explicitly confirms whether a Bangladesh ordinary-passport tourist must obtain the federal Somalia eVisa/ETAS, a separate Puntland VISO/local visa, both, or a different advance authorisation. The reported federal/regional conflict is material to boarding and entry. Keep the route **pending written confirmation**. Do not advise a Bangladeshi traveller to rely solely on the federal Somalia eVisa or on a reported VISO fee without written confirmation from both authorities and the operating carrier/airport.

### Stay, validity, fees and processing

- **Visa type / route:** federal eVisa/ETAS is the published federal route; a separate regional VISO/local visa is reported by secondary media only. Bangladesh ordinary-passport eligibility for the local route is not published.
- **Permitted stay / validity / entries:** not confirmed for the Puntland regional route or for a Bangladesh ordinary-passport tourist under VISO.
- **Government fee:** not confirmed by a primary Puntland source. The reported USD 60 amount is secondary reporting from November 2025 and must not be published as a current government fee.
- **Federal fee:** do not copy the federal Somalia fee into a Puntland record without confirming whether the federal authorisation is accepted and whether a separate local charge applies.
- **Processing time / payment method:** no verified regional information found.
- **Application channel / responsible office:** secondary reporting identifies Puntland's Ministry of Security as responsible for local foreigner entry/residence permissions. The official Ministry of Interior site publishes a general contact channel, but the exact VISO operator, official application portal and Bangladesh submission route are unconfirmed.
- **Passport validity:** the federal Somalia AIP states six months' validity from intended departure for entry into the Federal Republic of Somalia. Do not automatically treat this as a complete Puntland-specific checklist.
- **Documents / funds / accommodation / return travel / insurance:** no current official Puntland visitor checklist located. Do not infer the federal or neighbouring Somaliland checklist.
- **Transit:** no Bangladesh-specific Puntland transit exemption or separate airport transit rule located.
- **Border/airport scope:** Garowe and Bosaso are the reported practical entry points; the current official list of ports where VISO applies and whether the policy is enforced at both requires confirmation.
