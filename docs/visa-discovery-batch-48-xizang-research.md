# UniGo destination discovery batch 48 — Xizang / Tibet special travel regime

Checked: 15 November 2026  
Passport scope: Bangladesh ordinary passport  
Purpose: tourism / visitor  
Research outcome: **new non-ISO subnational destination candidate; research-only; do not create a separate visa requirement**

## Discovery and identity decision

Xizang (Tibet Autonomous Region) is a major practical visitor destination with additional regional access controls. The existing UniGo country master already represents China (CN), but repository search found no dedicated Tibet / Xizang travel-permit research record. Xizang is a subnational region of China, not a separate country or ISO 3166-1 destination. Track it as a special sub-destination under China, or as a structured permit note on the China record if UniGo does not support child destinations. Do not create an invented ISO code, country flag, or separate country-level visa record.

## Official regional-access evidence

The State Council Information Office's 28 March 2025 briefing on Xizang says the region is open to foreign visitors, but that management and safety measures apply because of its natural environment, geography and reception capacity. In response to a question about independent travel, the regional government representative said that free travel is not permitted within Xizang. This establishes that an ordinary China visitor visa alone should not be described as guaranteeing unrestricted independent travel throughout the region.

The National Immigration Administration's foreigner travel-permit guidance says:
- Foreigners visiting areas not open to foreigners may need a Foreigner's Travel Permit.
- For foreign tourist groups visiting non-open areas, travel agencies submit reception plans to the provincial public-security exit-entry administration 60 days before arrival.
- No approval is required for routes clear of prohibited areas; routes passing through prohibited areas require approval and supporting lists/passport/visa copies.

This is a national-level framework and is not a Bangladesh-specific Tibet application checklist. It does not establish that every Tibet itinerary requires the same permit or that every visitor must use one particular tour operator. The precise Tibet route, permitted areas and application channel require confirmation from the Xizang tourism / public-security authorities.

## Current regional travel alert

China's National Immigration Administration notice No. 5 of 2026, dated 27 August 2026, suspended issuance of the electronic border-administration-area permit for Shigatse City from 00:00 on 28 August 2026 after a major mudslide at the Nepal-side Gyirong Port disrupted road and communications. The notice asks holders of existing permits listing Shigatse to refrain from travelling there until further notice. No resumption notice was identified in this research pass.

This is a specific border-area permit suspension, not a general closure of the whole Xizang Autonomous Region. It must be checked again against the National Immigration Administration immediately before travel.

## Bangladesh ordinary-passport application context

The Chinese Embassy in Bangladesh's 9 April 2024 notice states that, from 11 April 2024, ordinary-passport applicants (including Mainland China, Hong Kong and Macao applications) submit through the Chinese Visa Center in Dhaka rather than directly to the Embassy. The Center is at 3rd Floor, Praasad Trade Center, 6 Kemal Ataturk Avenue, Banani, Dhaka.

The Embassy's 20 May 2026 notice says that, effective 5 July 2026, local Bangladeshi travel agencies handling Chinese visa applications on behalf of applicants must have IATA accreditation; applicants are advised to submit complete applications independently through the official Chinese Visa Application Service Center website. This notice concerns visa application intermediaries. It does not prove that an IATA-accredited Bangladeshi agency can issue or obtain Tibet travel permits.

The Embassy's fee schedule dated 14 May 2026 lists, for a Bangladesh passport, the following **Chinese visa** fees: single entry BDT 2,400 regular / 5,100 express / 6,400 urgent; double entry BDT 3,600 / 6,300 / 7,600; six-month multiple BDT 4,800 / 7,500 / 8,800; 12/24-month multiple BDT 7,200 / 9,900 / 11,200. These are China visa fees only. Chinese Visa Center service charges are separate. No official Tibet-specific permit fee or processing time was found.
