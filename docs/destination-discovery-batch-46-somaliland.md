# Somaliland destination discovery — research-only candidate

**Checked:** 15 November 2026  
**Destination type:** De facto territory with a separate immigration administration; not an ISO 3166-1 assigned country code  
**Passport/purpose:** Bangladesh ordinary passport, tourism/visitor

## Research result

The Somaliland Immigration and Border Control circular dated 14 September 2025 describes visa on arrival at Hargeisa International Airport (HGA) and Berbera Airport (BBO) for international travellers. The Somaliland Tourism Portal also states that international visitors arriving by air at those two airports can obtain a visa on arrival and that no eVisa/ETA or prior online authorisation is required for that air-arrival route. This is a general all-international-traveller rule, not a Bangladesh-specific nationality exemption.

The reviewed official guidance lists these arrival requirements:
- Passport valid for at least six months
- Return ticket
- Proof of funds, such as accessible cash or ATM access
- Local contact person and address or hotel details
- Payment of the applicable visa fee on arrival

For entry by land border or seaport, the guidance describes a separate advance entry-permit/visa process through a Somaliland mission. Do not apply the airport VOA rule to land or sea entry.

## Unresolved publication blockers

The accessible official sources do not state the exact government VOA fee or currency, permitted stay, visa validity, number of entries, processing time, detailed photo requirement or a complete Bangladesh-specific checklist. The Immigration website's current public pages do not provide a complete fee/stay schedule. These values must not be guessed.

Somaliland has no ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 or alpha-3 code assigned. Do not reuse Somalia's SO code for a separate Somaliland visa regime and do not invent an ISO code. A controlled internal destination identifier and flag-source policy would need approval before adding a production country row.

**Status: research-only.** No production country row, BGD tourism requirement or public permission statement is authorised from this batch.
