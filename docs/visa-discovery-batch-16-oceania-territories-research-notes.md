## Material findings and caveats

### U.S. visa bond — Guam and Northern Mariana Islands

The U.S. Department of State's current visa-bond page lists Bangladesh as subject to the visa-bond program from 21 January 2026. If a consular officer directs a bond, the amount is USD10,000, USD15,000 or USD20,000. This is a refundable bond, not a visa fee, and does not guarantee visa issuance. Bond holders must enter and exit through commercial air ports of entry. The bond is recorded separately from the USD185 MRV application fee and the Bangladesh reciprocity schedule's no-issuance-fee entry.

### American Samoa — extension conflict

The Department of Legal Affairs page says a one-time 30-day extension may be requested for USD50. The American Samoa Visitors Bureau table says the Other 30-Day permit cannot be extended. The base entry route, USD40 fee, sponsor, passport, ticket, police-clearance and 3–5-business-day requirements are supported by the Department of Legal Affairs. Extension availability is flagged as conflicting and should not be relied on without direct confirmation.

### French overseas territories — transit

France-Visas lists Bangladesh among nationalities requiring an airport-transit visa for transit through French airports, subject to exemptions. Its guidance states that the same rules apply to transit through international transit areas in French overseas territories. This is separate from the territory-specific entry visa.
