## Peru (PE)

### Confirmed findings

- Peru's Ministry of Foreign Affairs ordinary-passport visa list, published 5 March 2026, includes Bangladesh among nationalities requiring a visa. Do not apply the conditional US/Canada/UK/Australia/Schengen-visa exemption to Bangladeshi passport holders: the current Peruvian government tourist-visa guidance identifies that particular exemption for Chinese and Indian nationals, not Bangladesh.
- Bangladesh falls within the New Delhi Peruvian Consular Section's published consular jurisdiction. The New Delhi Embassy's current tourist-visa page specifically says nationals of Bangladesh, Nepal and Sri Lanka must provide a police clearance certificate.
- The Embassy says tourist-visa applications are submitted only at the Embassy of Peru in New Delhi. Applicants must personally submit a complete file at the premises, normally Monday to Friday from 10:00 to 12:00, excluding government holidays. An acknowledged complete file generates an email with an interview-scheduling link; email or courier delivery of isolated documents does not start processing.
- The tourist checklist states a passport valid for at least six months after expected arrival; two DGC-005 forms; passport copies; a valid personal email address and phone number; three recent colour photographs measuring 43 x 35 mm, white background, face occupying 70–80%; signed cover letter; employer NOC if employed; round-trip flight itinerary; day-by-day itinerary; hotel reservations; financial evidence even if sponsored; Form 16/ITR if applicable; sponsor guarantee/ID/financial evidence if sponsored; and a police-clearance certificate for Bangladesh nationals.
- The New Delhi page states tourist visas have 183-day validity and multiple entries, must be used within six months of issue, and that non-Indian applicants may take around 10 additional working days beyond the 7–10 working days quoted for Indian applicants after interview. This is an estimate, not a guarantee. The exact authorised stay must be read from the issued visa/admission record.
- The New Delhi page says the Embassy does not charge an additional fee beyond the official visa cost.

### Unresolved publication blockers

1. **Government fee conflict:** the current New Delhi tourist-visa page displays INR 3,000, while the older New Delhi tourist page and the official local consular tariff PDF display INR 2,550 (30 consular soles at INR 85 each). Both are official sources. No safe basis exists to choose one without written confirmation from the Embassy.
2. **Aadhaar requirement:** the New Delhi tourist checklist lists a copy of an Aadhaar card but does not explain what a Bangladesh-resident applicant without an Indian Aadhaar should submit. Do not tell applicants to obtain or substitute an unlisted document. Written Embassy confirmation of the accepted Bangladesh residence/identity alternative is required.
3. **Stay:** the New Delhi page confirms visa validity and multiple entries but does not state a Bangladesh-specific maximum stay. Preserve the general Peru tourist maximum only as background; do not represent 183-day visa validity as an automatically authorised 183-day stay.
4. **Transit:** the Embassy page says a transit visa has applied since 1 February 2025 to foreign nationals who require one. The current Bangladesh classification in the official transit-exemption list and the exact itinerary must be checked before giving a BGD-specific transit conclusion.

## Isle of Man (IM)

### Confirmed findings

- The Isle of Man is a Crown Dependency with its own Immigration Rules, similar to but not identical to UK rules. The UK Home Office fee calculator explicitly lists the **Standard Visitor visa for the UK, Isle of Man, Jersey and Guernsey**.
- The current Bangladesh Home Office fee calculator shows **BDT 23,110** for a short-term Standard Visitor visa (up to six months). This supersedes the older BDT 22,650 value in the pending record. Optional VFS services are separate.
- The Isle of Man Immigration Service currently estimates **up to 13 weeks** for visit applications from abroad. This is an estimate, not a guarantee, and is more conservative than the generic UK Standard Visitor processing estimate.
- The application is made online through UKVI, followed by a Visa Application Centre appointment. The Isle of Man Government says initial applications are handled through British diplomatic/VAC services and referred to the Isle of Man Immigration Service for decision.
- GOV.UK says the applicant's fingerprints and a digital photograph are taken at the VAC. The VFS Bangladesh page identifies the Dhaka centre. This confirms VAC photo capture; it does not prove that a printed photo is never required by a personalised checklist.
- The Isle of Man Government states that from 1 July 2026 successful Entry Clearance is recorded digitally instead of a passport vignette. It expressly says this digital record is **not an eVisa**.
- A visit is usually for up to six months, subject to the permission granted. The applicant must show a valid passport for the whole intended stay, travel dates, accommodation, trip cost/funds and relevant personal, employment or sponsor details. The application form determines further evidence; certified translations are required for documents not in English or Welsh.

### Unresolved publication blocker

The Isle of Man's current Immigration Rules page links to rules effective 2 November 2026 and says the Isle of Man rules are not identical to UK rules. The current PDF was linked and checked but the host rejected retrieval, so the current Isle of Man Appendix 2 visa-national list could not be read. An older official Isle of Man list includes Bangladesh and the current UK visa-national list includes Bangladesh, but those are not a substitute for checking the current Isle of Man Appendix. Keep the record pending until the current Appendix 2 Bangladesh entry is confirmed from an accessible official source or in writing by the Isle of Man Immigration Service.

### Photo and checklist result

- Bangladesh route: VFS Global Dhaka biometric appointment.
- GOV.UK confirms a digital facial photograph and fingerprints are taken at the VAC.
- No printed-photo dimensions, pixel dimensions, file size, file format or background standard is stated in the reviewed Bangladesh Standard Visitor route page. Do not label this “no fixed size published”; the personalised application checklist remains controlling.
- Status: **captured at VAC**; printed-photo requirement not stated.
