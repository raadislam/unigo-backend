# Bangladesh Schengen tourism checklist source audit

Checked: 15 November 2026  
Passport: Bangladesh ordinary passport  
Purpose: Short-stay Schengen tourism (Type C)  
Scope: All 29 Schengen states. This is a source and checklist audit, not a claim that every state has a Bangladesh VFS checklist.

## Schengen membership

The Council of the EU confirms 29 Schengen countries, including Bulgaria and Romania as full members from 1 January 2025: https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/policies/schengen-area/\n\nImportant source-quality warning: the EEAS Bangladesh travel page reviewed here still says there are 26 Schengen countries and incorrectly places Croatia, Bulgaria and Romania outside the area. It is therefore used only as a starting point for older Bangladesh representation arrangements, never as the source of current Schengen membership. Current membership is taken from the EU Council and national foreign ministries.

## Coverage status\n\n- 27 of 29 Schengen destinations have a Bangladesh VFS route or a verified short-stay representation/application route identified in this audit.\n- Bulgaria and Greece have direct VFS Bangladesh portals, but their dynamically served tourism checklist details were not extractable from the accessible page text. Bulgaria confirms a digital photo capture at the VAC.\n- Lithuania and Romania remain without a Bangladesh-specific VFS/competent-mission tourism checklist located in this review; keep their destination-specific checklist and photo fields unverified.\n\n## Verified photo details captured

- Germany / Estonia route: one recent biometric photograph, 35 × 45 mm, white background, face coverage 70–80%; the Estonia restriction above still applies.
- Denmark / Norway route: one colour photograph, 35 × 45 mm, light background, head length 30–36 mm, no older than six months.
- Netherlands / Poland route: 35 × 45 mm, colour, white or light background, no older than six months. VFS may capture the digital photo at the appointment; confirm whether a printed photo is needed for the appointment route.
- Sweden / Finland / Iceland / Latvia / Luxembourg / Slovenia route: one colour 35 × 45 mm photograph, full face, light background, no older than six months.
- Switzerland / Hungary / Liechtenstein route: one passport-size photograph, 35–40 mm width, white background, no older than six months.
- Italy / Malta / Slovakia route: two identical colour ICAO photographs, 40 × 35 mm, white background, taken within the last six months.
- Croatia: colour 35 × 45 mm photograph, neutral expression, light/white background, head about two-thirds of image and no higher than 36 mm. The current page does not state a maximum photo age.
- Belgium: VFS page says 35–45 mm width, face 70–80%, white background, no older than six months. It lists two photos for short-stay non-VIS cases and three for short-stay VIS cases. Follow the appointment-specific instruction.
- France / Austria / Czechia / Portugal: France-Visas generates the applicable personalised checklist; the Bangladesh VFS static page does not publish a complete country-specific printed-photo specification. Do not copy another country's measurements.
- Spain: Embassy guidance says recent colour passport-size photograph on a light background; the cited page does not publish an exact dimension or maximum age.
- Belgium: VFS states that a live digital photograph is captured at the appointment; the photo-count instruction is still route-dependent.
- Bulgaria: VFS confirms a digital photo is captured at the Bangladesh VAC, but the accessible portal does not state printed-photo dimensions or copy count.\n- Greece: the Bangladesh VFS portal is confirmed, but exact tourism photo specifications were not retrievable from the accessible page.\n- Lithuania and Romania: exact Bangladesh-route photo specifications were not verified. Keep them unverified rather than assuming 35 × 45 mm.

## Apostille and legalisation findings

1. Italy: the VFS tourism checklist requires English-translated and apostilled marriage certificates (including Nikah Nama / Hindu marriage act), divorce certificates and spouse death certificates for the corresponding applicant circumstances. It also requires apostilled parental consent, minor birth certificate and legal-guardianship evidence in the listed minor cases. One apostille per submitted document is specified. This is a country-specific requirement, not a general Schengen rule.
2. Croatia: VFS states that the Embassy may request additional documents, certified translations and international certification (apostille) during processing. Record as conditional / may be requested, not automatically required for every tourist.
3. Belgium: the VFS Bangladesh page links to the Belgian Embassy in New Delhi for document guidance and describes apostille issued by India's Ministry of External Affairs. That India-specific instruction must not be applied to Bangladeshi applicants.
4. Switzerland / Hungary / Liechtenstein: the current Swiss Dhaka tourism checklist requests civil-status evidence but does not state an apostille requirement in the reviewed checklist.
5. Germany: the January 2026 tourism checklist asks for translations into English or German and includes a notarised/legalised guardian-consent requirement in a narrow minor scenario; it does not create a general apostille rule.
6. Other countries: do not infer an apostille requirement from the Schengen common visa framework. Add one only where the competent mission's Bangladesh checklist expressly says so or requests it in an individual case.

Bangladesh's Apostille Convention status: the Convention entered into force for Bangladesh on 30 March 2025. Bangladesh's e-Apostille is issued by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. This does not mean every foreign mission requires an apostille for every visa document. Sources: https://www.hcch.net/en/news-archive/details/?varevent=1060 and https://mofa-servicedirectory.apostille.mygov.bd/eapostille

## Implementation safety

- Never label an unverified country checklist as verified.
- Preserve separate route and country-specific photo dimensions, count, age, background, capture-at-VAC and head-measurement details.
- Apostille must be an explicit checklist document only for the relevant country and applicant scenario; it must not be a universal Schengen item.
- Recheck the provider/mission page before every publication. VFS is a document collection provider, not the visa decision authority.
