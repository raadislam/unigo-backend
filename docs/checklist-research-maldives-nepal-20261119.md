# Checklist research candidates — Maldives and Nepal (Bangladesh ordinary passport)

Checked: 19 November 2026
Scope: tourism / ordinary passport / official sources. Research only. This note does not authorize import or publication.

## Maldives (MV)

### Verified route
- Tourist visa is granted on arrival; no pre-approval is required for tourists of all nationalities.
- Official Maldives Immigration entry requirements: MRZ passport with at least 1 month validity; confirmed return/onward journey; prepaid booking at a registered accommodation and sufficient funds, or approved sponsorship; meet onward-destination requirements; submit Traveller Declaration electronically within 96 hours before arrival; yellow-fever certificate if arriving from/routed through a risk country.
- Official Immigration page updated 10 September 2026.
- No tourist visa application, visa fee, visa-photo upload, or VAC capture requirement is stated in this route. Photo requirements should be recorded as not applicable to the tourist-visa application, not as “no photo ever required”.

### Sources
1. Maldives Immigration, Tourist Visa On Arrival: https://www.immigration.gov.mv/visa/tourist-visa (updated 10 Sep 2026)
2. Maldives Immigration legacy page: https://www2.immigration.gov.mv/tourist-visa/
3. Maldives Mission article (21 Jun 2026): https://mdvmission.gov.mv/jed/articles/maldives-visa-information-21

### Applicant checklist guidance (not separate immigration mandates)
Common entry evidence: passport; confirmed return/onward itinerary; prepaid registered accommodation booking; evidence of sufficient funds or approved sponsorship; onward-destination visa where applicable; completed IMUGA Traveller Declaration; yellow-fever certificate only where applicable.
Profile-specific supporting evidence should be labelled recommended/conditional rather than mandatory: employee—employment/leave evidence; business owner—business/tax evidence; student—enrolment/leave evidence; retired—pension/financial evidence. Do not imply these profile documents are formal Maldives visa requirements unless an official authority or a specific airline/entry officer requires them.

## Nepal (NP)

### Verified route
- Bangladesh nationals are eligible for tourist visa on arrival at Tribhuvan International Airport and other designated entry points.
- SAARC nationals (except Afghanistan) receive a gratis tourist visa for up to 30 days on the first visit in a visa year (1 January–31 December). Subsequent visits within the visa year are subject to fees.
- Official Embassy of Nepal, Dhaka says processing for applications is two working days, and lists supporting documents for visa applicants: visa form, passport copy, National ID or birth certificate, two-way flight booking, and hotel booking. These application documents should not be represented as mandatory for VOA unless the current arrival procedure requires them.
- Official Embassy fee schedule effective 1 April 2026 lists paid eTA rates after gratis eligibility: 15-day multiple entry Tk 3,630; 30-day Tk 6,050; 90-day Tk 15,125.

### Sources
1. Embassy of Nepal, Dhaka, Visa: https://bd.nepalembassy.gov.np/pages/visa-1/
2. Department of Immigration Nepal, Visa Information: https://www.immigration.gov.np/visa-information
3. Nepal Tourism Board, Tourist Visa: https://ntb.gov.np/en/plan-your-trip/before-you-come/tourist-visa

### Applicant checklist guidance (not separate immigration mandates)
Common entry evidence: passport; completed online arrival/visa form as applicable; arrival visa/gratis eligibility; travel itinerary; accommodation and funds evidence as appropriate. First SAARC visit in visa year: gratis eligibility evidence/status; later visit: fee evidence. Applicant profile documents (employment/NOC, business evidence, student evidence, pension/financial evidence) should be recommended/conditional, not falsely presented as a Nepal government checklist.
