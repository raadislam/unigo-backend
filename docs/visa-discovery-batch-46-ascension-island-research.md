# Batch 46 — Ascension Island discovery and research

**Checked:** 15 November 2026  
**Destination code:** AC (exceptionally reserved alpha-2 code)  
**Parent jurisdiction:** Saint Helena, Ascension and Tristan da Cunha (UK Overseas Territory)  
**Regional coverage:** Africa — South Atlantic & Polar Territories  
**Status:** New destination candidate; official visitor-entry framework researched; Bangladesh ordinary-passport eligibility remains unconfirmed; not approved for visa publication.

## Discovery outcome

Ascension Island is a distinct destination with its own Entry Control Ordinance and eVisa system. It is not safe to reuse Saint Helena or Tristan da Cunha entry rules. The ISO 3166/MA's official 2011 subdivision notice identifies Ascension as SH-AC and Tristan da Cunha as SH-TA. An official copy of the ISO 3166-1 reserved-code list also records AC for Ascension Island. UniGo leaves the alpha-3 field null rather than treating a reserved alpha-3 element as an assigned code. The existing UniGo master did not contain a separate AC destination row at the start of this batch.

The candidate is retained as research-only until the production master row is added through the guarded country-master workflow and the Bangladesh-specific nationality result is confirmed. No Bangladesh tourism visa requirement is created by this batch.

## Official entry framework

- The Ascension Island Government immigration page says travellers require an entry visa unless exempt by a narrow occupational category. It asks applicants to apply at least 14 days before intended travel and requires a passport with at least six months' validity.
- The official Ascension eVisa FAQ says there is no visa-on-arrival facility and that all nationalities, including British citizens, require an eVisa except the listed military, Crown-service, government, NATO and qualifying dependant/crew exemptions.
- The visitor eVisa allows a single stay of up to three months, with a cumulative maximum of three months in any 12-month period. Employment is not permitted on a visitor eVisa without prior permission.
- The official 2026 fee schedule sets the visitor fee at GBP 40 standard or GBP 60 fast track, with GBP 10 for applicants under 12. The standard fee applies when applying at least 14 days before arrival; fast track is for shorter notice or priority consideration.
- The FAQ says the authority aims to determine 90% of applications within 21 days; complex cases may take up to six weeks. Fast-track applications are aimed to be assessed within 14 days. These are service aims, not guarantees.
- The official FAQ requires evidence of adequate medical/travel insurance and specifies at least GBP 500,000 medical-evacuation cover. The older official visitor help sheet asks for a confirmed accommodation arrangement and insurance details. The UK Government's entry-requirements summary also lists a return ticket, accommodation, sufficient funds and medical insurance, but expressly says its summary is based on a full British citizen passport. Do not present that UK summary as a complete Bangladesh-specific checklist without Ascension confirmation.
- The official eVisa application requires a clear-face JPEG upload. The FAQ says the image need not be passport-photo quality and does not publish fixed printed dimensions, pixel dimensions or a file-size limit. This confirms a digital upload instruction only; it does not prove that a printed photograph can never be requested.
- Applications are made directly through the official Ascension eVisa portal. The Government says no third-party firms are permitted to handle eVisa applications.

## Bangladesh ordinary-passport classification — unresolved

The current Ascension eVisa FAQ lists Belarus, China, Egypt, Hong Kong, Iran, Libya, Macau, North Korea, Russia, Syria, Taiwan, Ukraine and Vietnam as nationalities to which the Government does not issue eVisas. Bangladesh is not in that published list. However, the live application portal asks applicants to select their nationality, and the exact result for a Bangladeshi ordinary passport was not retrievable from the public page. Absence from the exclusion list is not recorded as a confirmed positive selector result.

The UK Home Office's current Appendix Visitor: Visa National List, updated 29 October 2026, includes Bangladesh. Ascension's eVisa FAQ says nationals who require a UK visa may be required to show a multi-entry UK visa valid for both the outward and return journeys before an Ascension eVisa is issued. The FAQ says this does not apply to travellers going to and from the Falkland Islands who do not leave the airhead. The wording is conditional ("may be required"), so UniGo must not convert it into an unconditional rule without confirmation.

**Exact confirmation required:** obtain written confirmation from the Ascension Island Government / Immigration Office that (1) a holder of a Bangladeshi ordinary passport can select Bangladesh and submit a visitor eVisa application, and (2) whether the multi-entry UK visa condition applies to all Bangladesh applicants or only particular UK transit/route arrangements, including travel through South Africa and the Falkland Islands.

Until then, keep the Bangladesh tourism record pending and unpublished. Do not infer visa-free, eVisa-eligible, transit-exempt or visa-on-arrival status.

## Photo and checklist status

- **Digital photo:** clear-face JPEG upload is confirmed by the official FAQ and visitor help sheet.
- **Fixed size:** no fixed printed or digital dimensions are stated in the reviewed official guidance.
- **Printed photo:** not established as unnecessary; the official instructions describe a digital upload but do not expressly rule out a later request for a printed photo.
- **Other route details:** six-month passport validity, medical/travel insurance and at least GBP 500,000 evacuation cover are published. Return ticket, confirmed accommodation and sufficient funds appear in the UK Government's summary of local Ascension rules, but its stated passport basis is British citizen; confirm Bangladesh applicability with Ascension before treating these as a mandatory Bangladesh-specific checklist.
- **VAC:** no Bangladesh VAC or intermediary is identified; applications are made directly to the official Ascension eVisa portal.

## Data and publication decision

- **Destination discovery:** new candidate identified.
- **Official-source research:** visitor eVisa framework researched.
- **Bangladesh-specific research:** incomplete; live nationality selector and UK-visa condition need confirmation.
- **Country master:** create an unpublished AC row only after the guarded backup/restore workflow succeeds.
- **Bangladesh tourism requirement:** do not create or publish.
- **Photo record:** retain as research-only; do not mark a Bangladesh route's photo checklist verified until eligibility and route are confirmed.
