**Parent jurisdiction:** Somalia (SO) for ISO / internationally recognised country-master purposes; Somaliland is a self-administered territory with a distinct de facto immigration and border-control regime.

**Research outcome:** new practical non-ISO destination candidate; research-only; do not create or publish a standalone country-level visa requirement.

## Discovery and identity decision

Somaliland operates a distinct immigration and border-control system in practice. Its Immigration and Border Control authority publishes its own visa circulars and entry-permit process, and its Ministry of Civil Aviation and Airports Development has issued a separate airport visa-on-arrival statement. The current Somaliland tourism portal says that visas and travel authorisations issued by Somalia are not valid for entry into Somaliland.

The existing UniGo country master represents Somalia as ISO `SO`. Somaliland has no separate ISO 3166-1 assignment. Keep the Somalia country record unchanged and track Somaliland as a non-ISO child destination. Do not merge Somaliland's local entry rules into the Somalia country-level visa requirement: the two authorities' rules conflict in a way that is material to travellers.

## Bangladesh ordinary-passport tourism assessment

### Entry route — material official-source conflict

1. The Somaliland Immigration and Border Control Commissioner's circular dated **3 March 2025** divides travellers into Category A and Category B. Category A includes diplomatic/UN/national-service passports, specified multilateral/registered-NGO and invited categories, Somali-origin travellers with foreign travel documents, and a named list of nationalities/regions (European countries, Canada, the USA, Australia, New Zealand, China, Brazil, Taiwan, Russia, Turkey, South Africa, Morocco, Tunisia, IGAD and GCC members). Bangladesh ordinary-passport holders are not named in that Category A list and do not meet the listed passport/occupational categories. The circular says all other applicants, regardless of nationality, must apply in advance for the relevant tourist or other visa through Immigration Headquarters, local agents or a Somaliland diplomatic mission. Read literally, a Bangladeshi ordinary-passport tourist falls into Category B.
2. A later official Ministry of Civil Aviation and Airports Development press statement dated **9 September 2025** says all international travellers arriving at Egal International Airport and other airports under Somaliland jurisdiction continue to receive visa on arrival, without an electronic application or prior authorisation. It also says external authorities' visas/ETAs are not valid for Somaliland.
3. The current Ministry of Trade & Tourism / Department of Tourism & Archaeology tourism portal says visa on arrival is available at **Hargeisa Egal International Airport (HGA)** and **Berbera International Airport (BBO)**. It lists a six-month passport-validity condition, onward/return ticket, proof of sufficient funds, local contact or accommodation, and payment of the applicable fee at the port of entry. It says foreign nationals entering by land or sea may apply in advance for a Somaliland Entry Permit Visa through a Somaliland diplomatic mission.
4. A November 2025 SIBC directive reported by local media also says Somalia-issued visas are not recognised and that visa on arrival remains available at HGA and BBO, with advance Entry Permit Visas available through Somaliland missions. The primary signed directive was not retrievable during this research; treat the report as corroboration, not as the sole authority.
5. The current Somaliland immigration portal's application page also displays a tourist-visa option, but its own page footer expressly states that fees and details shown are placeholders for demonstration. The forms page similarly labels the downloadable entry-visa form a sample for demonstration. Do not treat the portal's displayed visa duration, fee, or processing estimate as real policy.

**Bangladesh-specific conclusion:** the newer general airport statements point toward visa on arrival for international travellers at HGA/BBO, while the SIBC March 2025 circular places nationalities not in its Category A list into an advance-application category. No reviewed primary source explicitly names a Bangladesh ordinary passport or clearly explains how the later airport policy supersedes the Category A/Category B circular for Bangladesh. Keep the route **pending written confirmation**. Do not advise a Bangladeshi traveller to board relying only on a Somalia eVisa or on an assumed Somaliland visa on arrival.

### Stay, validity, fees and processing

- **Maximum permitted stay / visa validity / entries:** not confirmed for a Bangladesh ordinary-passport tourist. The immigration portal displays “30 days” on a sample/demo service screen, but the same portal explicitly says displayed details are placeholders. Do not import 30 days as a verified stay or validity.
- **Government visa fee:** not confirmed. The portal's displayed USD 20 tourist-visa amount is expressly a placeholder and must not be published as an actual fee.
- **Processing time:** not confirmed. The portal's displayed two-day estimate and paid express/urgent options are expressly demonstration placeholders.
- **Payment method:** not confirmed for Bangladesh ordinary-passport applicants. The tourism portal says the applicable visa-on-arrival fee is paid at the port, but does not state the amount or accepted payment method.
- **Application channel:** unresolved for Bangladesh because of the Category A/Category B versus later airport-visa statements. The portal lists HGA/BBO airport arrival and advance Entry Permit Visas through missions for land/sea routes, but Bangladesh-specific eligibility needs SIBC confirmation.
- **Responsible mission / Bangladesh submission point / appointment:** no Somaliland mission or authorised application channel in Bangladesh was confirmed. The circular lists examples of missions in London, Dubai, Nairobi and Addis Ababa; this is not proof of jurisdiction over Bangladesh residents.
- **Passport validity:** the current tourism portal states at least six months on the date of entry. It is not a Bangladesh-specific checklist and should be re-confirmed with SIBC before publication.
- **Return/onward travel, funds and accommodation/contact:** the current tourism portal lists these as general airport-VOA conditions, with an exception for Somali-origin/diaspora travellers. Do not infer that the diaspora exception applies to an ordinary Bangladeshi passport holder.
- **Land/sea entry:** the tourism portal says advance Entry Permit Visa through a Somaliland diplomatic mission may be used for land and seaport entry. Exact Bangladesh application process and accepted crossing points require confirmation.
- **External Somalia documents:** the current Somaliland tourism portal states that Somalia-issued visas/ETAs are not valid for Somaliland. Somalia's own ICA announced a federal eVisa/ETAS requirement from 1 September 2025, but that federal authorisation must not be presented as a Somaliland entry document.
- **Transit:** no Bangladesh-specific Somaliland transit exemption or airside-transit rule was confirmed. Do not infer one from Somalia's federal eVisa policy.

## Exact blockers and next actions

1. Obtain written confirmation from Somaliland Immigration and Border Control that a **Bangladesh ordinary-passport holder travelling for tourism** may receive visa on arrival at HGA and/or BBO, or must apply in advance under Category B.
2. Ask SIBC to identify the current controlling circular and explain whether the 9 September 2025 Ministry airport statement superseded the 3 March 2025 Category A/Category B circular for all nationalities.
3. Confirm Bangladesh applicants' responsible mission, advance-application channel, required documents, appointment/submission process and any route-specific transit conditions.
4. Obtain the current official government fee, accepted payment method, visa validity, permitted stay, number of entries and published processing estimate; do not use the portal's demonstration placeholders.
5. Obtain the current Bangladesh-route photo instruction and determine whether a printed photo, digital upload, biometric capture or no photo is required.
6. Keep this candidate research-only until the route is confirmed and UniGo has an approved parent-child destination schema. Do not change the Somalia country record or create a separate country/visa/source row.
